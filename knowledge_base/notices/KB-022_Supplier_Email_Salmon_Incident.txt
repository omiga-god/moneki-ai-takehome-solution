Dear Xu Min,

Following our call this afternoon, I am confirming in writing what happened to
the salmon shipment that was scheduled to reach your central kitchen on the
morning of 4 July 2026, and what we are doing about it.

1. WHAT HAPPENED

The consignment left our Shanghai cold store at 21:10 on 3 July 2026 in truck
HU-8842. The refrigeration unit on that truck failed in transit during the
night. The driver noticed the alarm only at the second rest stop, and by then
the on-board logger had recorded a cargo temperature above 8 degrees Celsius
for 6 hours continuously. Our standard for this product is 0 to 4 degrees
Celsius at all times, so the cold chain was broken well beyond any tolerance
we are willing to argue about.

2. WHAT WE DID

Your receiving team measured the core temperature on arrival and refused the
delivery on the spot. That was the correct decision and we fully support it.
All 3 pallets were rejected and returned to us; none of the product was
re-routed to any other customer. The whole consignment has been destroyed and
the destruction certificate is attached to this mail.

3. COMMERCIAL SETTLEMENT

We will issue a credit note of CNY 8,600 against your July account. This
covers the full value of the rejected consignment. There is nothing for you to
pay and nothing for you to claim separately. The credit note will be raised on
6 July and should appear on your statement the same week.

4. SUPPLY RESUMPTION

We cannot cover your salmon requirement from the current stock rotation. The
next qualified lot arrives in our cold store on 11 July, and after quality
release we will be able to deliver to you again from 13 July 2026. I know this
leaves a gap of about a week in your menu and I am sorry for it. Please treat
13 July as firm; I will call you myself if anything moves.

5. CORRECTIVE ACTION

Effective immediately, every truck carrying product to your stores will run two
independent temperature loggers instead of one, with the second logger placed
inside the pallet rather than in the cabin. Readings will be sent to your
procurement mailbox before the truck leaves our yard and again on arrival. The
failed unit on HU-8842 has been taken out of service pending overhaul, and we
have brought forward the servicing schedule for the rest of the fleet.

I am available on my mobile all weekend if you want to go through any of this.
Again, my apologies for the disruption to your stores.
